# Project rules

TASK.md holds the owner's bounded grant and acceptance criteria. STATE.md records
current evidence and cannot expand that grant. These rules govern preservation
and completion. A proposal is not approval.

Preserve unrelated edits. Candidate preparation is not verification. Completion
requires the specified local check followed by review from someone other than
the candidate author, with a recorded result. Stop for owner direction if the
candidate needs a change outside TASK.md. No network or publication is authorized.
