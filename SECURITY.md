# Security and Responsible Disclosure Policy

*Submit a vulnerability:* Vulnerability reports can be submitted through [Bugcrowd](https://bugcrowd.com/cms-vdp). Reports may be submitted anonymously. If you share contact information, we will acknowledge receipt of your report within 3 business days.

Include the affected file or revision, what you found and how to reproduce the
problem where applicable. Keep sensitive details out of public issues. Ordinary
documentation questions can go to the
[issue tracker](https://github.com/DSACMS/indelegible/issues).

## Repository scope

Indelegible contains readable guidance, task and recovery conventions, synthetic
evidence and static metadata, schema and secret-pattern files. It has no deployed
service or owned executable validation stack.

CMS OSPO maintains the repository and uses authorized, bounded document, privacy
and secret checks when needed. There are no owned hosted workflows or scheduled
scans. The acknowledgement above comes from the inherited CMS disclosure policy.
The [current review packet](REVIEW.md#human-outbound-review-packet) records local
preparation and the human review still to be completed.

## Disclosure policy

The Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services is committed to ensuring the security of the American public by protecting their information from unwarranted disclosure. We want security researchers to feel comfortable reporting vulnerabilities they have discovered so we can fix them and keep our users safe. We developed our disclosure policy to reflect our values and uphold our sense of responsibility to security researchers who share their expertise with us in good faith.

Consult the [official CMS vulnerability disclosure policy](https://www.cms.gov/about-cms/information-systems/privacy/vulnerability-disclosure-policy)
for authorization and scope. These links do not authorize testing third-party
infrastructure. Use the policy's listed scope contact when uncertain.

Review the HHS Disclosure Policy and websites in scope:
[https://www.hhs.gov/vulnerability-disclosure-policy/index.html](https://www.hhs.gov/vulnerability-disclosure-policy/index.html).

This policy describes *what systems and types of research* are covered under this
policy, *how to send* us vulnerability reports, and *how long* we ask security
researchers to wait before publicly disclosing vulnerabilities.
